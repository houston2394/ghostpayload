This is the first entry. If you’re reading it, the site is live — which means the
plan survived contact with reality at least once.

I spend most of my time somewhere between **OSINT, red teaming, and AI agents**.
Those three overlap more than people expect: all of them are really about turning
scattered, public signal into something you can act on. A flight transponder, a
certificate-transparency log, a seismic feed — individually they’re trivia.
Correlated, they’re a picture.

## What this journal is for

Two things:

1. **A record of the build.** I want the dead ends written down while they still
   sting, not sanded smooth after the fact.
2. **A forcing function.** It’s harder to hand-wave past a problem when you’ve
   committed to explaining it to someone else.

## What’s next

The near-term focus is [ShadowBroker](/projects) — pulling open intelligence into
one interface and letting agents hunt for correlations across domains that don’t
normally talk to each other. I’ll write about the architecture as it firms up:
the ingestion layer, how the agents are scoped, and where passive recon ends and
something louder begins.

More soon. `— H`
